Research & Technical Work

FSAE · Design Calculations

Brake Pedal Design, Penn Electric Racing

Worked through a full brake pedal design from the FSAE rulebook: derived weight transfer and required tire friction force from vehicle dynamics, sized the pedal ratio and master cylinder bore against 1200/400 psi front/rear targets, and carried a 1500 N structural design load (per Rule T.3.2.2) through the pushrod and pedal structure. Result: pedal ratio of 6.0, 0.578 in master cylinder bore, and a validated hydraulic margin of roughly 2× over the minimum required pressure.

Published Research · SSRN

Microstructured Films for AI Thermal Control on CubeSats

With Bridget Kimsey, NASA STEM Enhancement in Earth Science (SEES). Proposed a bioinspired PDMS microstructured film, modeled on Saharan silver ant hairs, that reflects roughly 95% of solar UV and visible radiation while emitting heat through the 8 to 13 micron infrared band, giving CubeSats passive radiation shielding and thermal control for onboard AI hardware without added power draw.
